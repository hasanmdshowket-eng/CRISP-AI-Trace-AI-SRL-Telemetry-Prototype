📚 Scientific Reference & Guidelines Bank

Universal methodological guidelines and research quality standards.

📋 1. Claim–Evidence–Reasoning (CER) Framework for Scientific Inquiry

Claim: A direct, precise statement answering the empirical question or hypothesis.

Evidence: Measurable, quantitative or qualitative data (means, variances, replication $n$, effect sizes) substantiating the claim.

Reasoning: Explicit scientific mechanism explaining why the evidence supports the claim using domain laws, chemical reactions, physical principles, or biological models.

🧪 2. Experimental Design & Statistical Planning Standards

Variable Isolation: Clearly distinguish manipulated independent variables from measured dependent variables.

Replication & Power: Plan sufficient independent experimental units ($n \ge 3$ per cell for bench trials; $n \ge 30$ for observational/evaluative pilots).

Inferential Mapping: Pre-specify parametric tests (ANOVA, $t$-tests) with assumption fallbacks (Welch, Mann–Whitney, Kruskal–Wallis).

⚠️ 3. Research Ethics, Quality Assurance & Data Governance

Versioned Data Pipeline: Maintain strict separation between RAW unmanipulated observations, CLEAN pre-processed files, and ANALYSIS scripts.

Safety Protocol: Document physical hazard mitigations, chemical disposal, or human-subject consent/assent procedures.
